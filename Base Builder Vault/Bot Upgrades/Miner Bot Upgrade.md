[[Stone]]
[[Polymer]]
[[Pyrite]]
[[Cassiterite]]

[[Bauxite]]
[[Gold]]

[[Ilmenite]]

20 [[Electronic Module]] + 25 [[Control Unit]] +  30 [[Precision Gear]] + 15 [[Advanced Bot Frame]]
