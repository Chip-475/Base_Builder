The player begin with x [[Miner Bot]] x [[Worker Bot]] and 1 [[Builder Bot]], the first [[Assembler]] and [[Furnace mk1]] are free to build, he has to mine [[Stone]] and make [[Stone Brick]].It has to mine [[Pyrite]] and he will smelt it to make [[Iron]] and make [[Iron Plate]] and [[Iron Rod]].Then it would use it to smelt [[Copper]],  with [[Iron]] the player can make [[Iron Gear]] and [[Iron Casing]] that will be used to make the [[Alloy Furnace]] that will introduce [[Bronze]] at this point the player will have to build a copper extraction line and a tin extraction line.
Now the player can make the [[Press]] and use it to make next level items like: [[Reinforced Plate]], [[Machine Frame]], [[Copper Plate]], [[Bronze Casing]], [[Advanced Frame]].
Now the player can start building new electric networks with [[Generator mk1]] and [[Power Pole]] with copper products.
The player will mine [[Coal]] to fuel the Generator.
The last step of beginning era will be to assemble  the [[Electric Workbench]] and [[Circuit Board]] that will be needed for [[Motherboard]] that will be necessary for future buildings.
